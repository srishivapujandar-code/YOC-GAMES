How to Play LinkedIn Zip

Master the logic rules of the tournament

1

Start on Number 1

Every puzzle begins at the circle marked 1. Drag or click from here to begin tracing your path.

2

Connect Numbers in Ascending Order

Your path must pass through the numbered checkpoints in strictly sequential order (1 → 2 → 3 → ...). You cannot skip numbers or visit higher numbers early.

3

Cover Every Single Square

To win, your single continuous line must visit 100% of the squares on the grid. No unvisited squares can remain!

4

Respect Barrier Walls

The thick red lines on the board are interior walls. Your path cannot cross through walls or move diagonally.

5

Backtracking & Shortcuts

Made a wrong turn? Drag backwards over your line, click any previous visited cell, or press Undo (Z) to roll back.
